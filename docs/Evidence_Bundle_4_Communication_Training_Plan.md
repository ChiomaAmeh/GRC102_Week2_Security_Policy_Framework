# Evidence Bundle 4: Communication and Training Plan

## Rollout Timeline

### Week 1

- Executive Announcement
- Policy Publication

### Weeks 1-4

- Training Delivery
- Awareness Campaigns
  
### Weeks 4-5

- Attestation Collection

### Week 6

- Compliance Review
- Escalation Activities

---

## Communication Plan

| Audience | Message | Channel | Owner | Timeline |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Employees | AUP requirements, MFA, cloud storage restrictions | LMS, Email, All Hands | HR / Security | Weeks 1–3 |
| IT Staff | SOP compliance, ticketing requirements | Workshops | IT Manager | Weeks 2–4 |
| Managers | Approval responsibilities | Briefings | HR | Weeks 1–2 |
| Contractors | AUP adherence | Vendor Portal | Legal | Weeks 3–6 |

---

## Success Measures

- >98% policy attestation
- <2% assessment failures
- 100% ticket-based provisioning
- Zero unauthorised access

---

## Escalation Process

### First Occurrence (7 Days)

- Reminder sent
  
### Second Occurrence (14 Days)

- Coaching session
  
### Third Occurrence (21+ Days)

- HR escalation
- Access restrictions
