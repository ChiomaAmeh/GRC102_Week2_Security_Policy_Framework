## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Acceptable Use Policy is to define acceptable, ethical, and secure use of NexusTech information assets, computing resources, networks, cloud services, and business data.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Temporary workers
- Vendors
- Third-party partners

---

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 Acceptable Use

Corporate systems are provided for legitimate business purposes, and users must exercise due care in protecting company assets.

### 3.2 Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

- Install unapproved software
- Conduct unauthorised network scanning
- Share copyrighted material illegally
- Send spam
- Transmit offensive or harassing content

### 3.3 Data Protection

Corporate data must be stored only on approved repositories such as:

- SharePoint
- OneDrive
- Approved AWS storage
  
Personal cloud storage services are prohibited.

### 3.4 Account Protection

- Accounts must never be shared
- Credentials must remain confidential
- MFA is mandatory
  
### 3.5 Personal Use

Reasonable personal use is allowed provided it:

- Does not interfere with work
- Does not consume excessive resources
- Does not create security risks
  
---

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

### Information Security Manager

- Maintains policy
- Oversees awareness training
- Reviews exceptions
  
### IT Team

- Implements controls
- Monitors compliance
  
### Managers

- Enforce policy
- Approve requests
- Escalate issues
  
### HR

- Manage attestations
- Coordinate disciplinary actions
  
### Employees

- Read
- Understand
- Comply
- Report incidents
  
---

## 5. Compliance and Enforcement

Failure to comply may result in:

- Disciplinary action
- Access revocation
- Employment termination
- Legal action

---

## 6. Exceptions

Exception requests:

- Must be submitted formally
- Require risk assessment
- Require compensating controls
- Expire within 12 months
  
---

## 7. Review and Approval

Approved by:

- CEO
- Security Steering Committee
  
Review Frequency:

- Annual
- Following major incidents
- Following major technology changes
