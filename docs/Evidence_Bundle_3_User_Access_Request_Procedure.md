# Evidence Bundle 3: User Access Request Procedure

## Purpose

Provide a formal and auditable process for access requests and provisioning.

---

## Scope

Applies to:

- Helpdesk Technicians
- System Administrators
- Cloud Platforms
- ERP Systems
- Business Applications

---

## Step 1 - Formal Request Intake

Managers submit requests through the ITSM Portal.

Informal requests are rejected.

---

## Step 2 - Verification

Verify:

- Directory account exists
- NDA exists
- Security training completed

---

## Step 3 - Approval Verification

Confirm approvals from:

- Line Manager
- System Owner

---

## Step 4 - Least Privilege Review

Validate access against RBAC standards.

Escalate unusual requests.

---

## Step 5 - Provisioning

Provision account.

Apply:

- MFA
- Least privilege
- Temporary password

---

## Step 6 - Notification

Notify:

- User
- Line Manager
  
Provide access instructions.

---

## Step 7 - Evidence Capture

Record:

- Approvals
- RBAC assignments
- Provisioning evidence
  
Archive for audit purposes.

---

## Step 8 - Emergency Access

Emergency access:

- Requires approval
- Uses temporary privilege
- Expires within 12 hours
  
---

## Step 9 - Retention

Retain records:

- Minimum 7 years

For:

- ISO 27001
- SOC 2
- HIPAA
- PCI
